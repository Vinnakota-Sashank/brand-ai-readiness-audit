# Field Research Methodology

The research layer compares website structure with AI citation behavior across 150+ Indian brands in 25 categories. The working corpus includes ChatGPT and Gemini responses, site observations, and a structured citation workbook; Perplexity-specific patterns are recorded where available.

## Collection Design

1. Select categories spanning D2C, local businesses, SaaS, services, and established brands.
2. Include large and small brands within each category to expose authority and scale effects.
3. Ask discovery, comparison, and branded fact-extraction questions.
4. Record citations, omissions, inaccurate facts, homepage depth, rendering mode, pricing visibility, structured data, and authority links.
5. Compare observed site signals with citation outcomes and inspect false positives before changing severity.

## Calibration Rules

The research informs prioritization, not certainty. Retrieval blocks and directly observed contradictions can support strong findings. Missing markup, weak CTA signals, and outbound citation gaps need scoped evidence and may become advisories when intent is unknown. The implementation retains limitations because observational correlation is not a causal guarantee.

## Reproducibility

Raw research notes remain at the workspace root. The audit package contains the synthesized findings and severity calibration used by the runbooks so an evaluator can trace a recommendation back to the measurement model.
